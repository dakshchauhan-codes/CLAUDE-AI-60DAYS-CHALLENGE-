Built something this week I'm genuinely proud of — a Brain Dump Action Planner skill. 🧠⚡

🔨 Skill Name: Brain Dump Action Planner

⚙️ What It Does:
Paste in any messy input — meeting transcript, voice memo, rough notes — and it outputs a fully self-contained interactive HTML dashboard with:
→ Tabbed navigation (Overview / Actions / Speakers / Risks / Decisions)
→ Color-coded priority badges (🔴 High / 🟠 Medium / 🟢 Low)
→ Checkable action items with owners & deadlines
→ Conflicts, blockers, and open questions clearly separated
→ Speaker attribution for every key point

📚 Key Learnings from Building This:
1. Output format matters as much as output content — a table people can act on beats a paragraph they have to re-read.
2. Never invent, assume, or fill gaps — only surface what's explicitly there. Trust the source.
3. Conflicts and open questions deserve their own section. Most summaries bury them.
4. A skill that works on a messy quarterly review transcript will work on almost anything.

Tested it on a real quarterly leadership meeting — 7 participants, 2 pages of transcript — and it extracted 6 action items, 5 decisions, 4 open questions, and 3 conflicts cleanly.

The goal was simple: make the output feel like Notion, Asana, or Linear — not a wall of text.

Mission accomplished. 💪
