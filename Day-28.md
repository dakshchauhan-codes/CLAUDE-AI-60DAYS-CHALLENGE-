🚀 Day 28 of #60DayClaudeAIChallenge

🏥 Built AdmitSim — a Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator as a single HTML file.
No backend. No framework. Just Claude API + Tailwind CSS + Vanilla JS.
What it simulates:
→ PA Workflow branches (Approved / Pending / Denied + Appeal)
→ Real-time readiness score across 6 weighted components
→ CMS 2-Midnight Rule & MOON notification alerts
→ InterQual / Milliman clinical documentation triggers for Acute MI & CHF
→ 9-step Admission Timeline with live milestone tracking
→ Care Coordination Team cards (UR, Case Manager, Discharge Planner)
→ Risk Tracking — Documentation, Insurance, Bed, Clinical
→ CMS Governance Benchmarks (unlocked at ≥75%)
→ Final Admit ✅ or Not Ready ⚠ decision at 90% threshold
Score starts at 30–60%. You take actions, resolve PA, assign beds, complete consent — and work toward admission clearance.
Denied PA + ICU = hard cap at 69%. Can't admin your way out of a clinical denial.
Built this as a training environment for hospital admission coordinators to practice the full workflow before going live.
