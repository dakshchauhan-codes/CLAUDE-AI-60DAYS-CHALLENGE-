🚀 Day 25 of #60DaysClaudeAIChallenge

🚁 Just pitched my startup idea — Aerial Resource Delivery for Inaccessible Regions — on an AI Shark Tank simulator.

The concept: an autonomous drone delivery network that brings medicine, vaccines, and critical supplies to communities cut off by mountains, floods, forests, and conflict zones. Places where roads don't exist and lives depend on what can be delivered from the air.

📊 The AI panel scored it 61/100 and said "Come Back Later" — which honestly feels like the right kind of fuel.

Key feedback:
• Business model needs sharper unit economics
• Go-to-market strategy needs more clarity
• Execution plan needs to be more concrete

But the core problem is real — billions of people globally are cut off from critical resources, and drone delivery could change that.

This is just the beginning. Refining the model, strengthening the numbers, and coming back stronger. 💪
