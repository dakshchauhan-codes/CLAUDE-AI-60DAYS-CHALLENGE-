🚀 Day 30 of #60DayClaudeAIChallenge

Just built something I'm genuinely excited about:
 A Supply Chain Builder simulation 🔗📦
Ever wondered what actually happens when you choose "cheap supplier" over "reliable supplier," or "fast shipping" over "sustainable shipping"? I turned that question into an interactive tool.
Here's the concept: you're handed a randomly generated company — mine was Crest Collective, a carbon road frame bike brand selling across Germany, France, Spain & Italy — and you make 5 real-world supply chain decisions: supplier strategy, factory location, warehouse network, transportation method, and inventory approach.
The twist? Five live business metrics shift in real time after every single choice — Cost Efficiency, Delivery Speed, Risk Level, Customer Satisfaction, and Sustainability. No decision is free. Every "win" somewhere quietly costs you somewhere else.
By the end, you get a full performance dashboard with an overall score, strengths, weaknesses, and concrete improvement tips — just like a real ops review. My run scored 53/100 — solid on risk management and customer satisfaction, but cost efficiency and delivery speed dragged the score down hard. Turns out picking sea freight to save money has consequences. 😅
It's been such a great way to actually feel trade-offs instead of just reading about them in a case study. Supply chain strategy isn't about finding the "right" answer — it's about knowing which trade-offs your business can afford.
Would love to hear how others would balance these five levers 👇
