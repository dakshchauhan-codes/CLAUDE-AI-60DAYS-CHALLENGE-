🚀  Day 45 of #60DayClaudeAIChallenge

I kept starting things. And quitting them 3-4 days in. Every time.
So instead of just "trying harder" again, I turned it into a decision problem and had Claude run me through a full decision framework — options, hidden biases, a 7-dimension scoring matrix, even a premortem on how each choice could fail 12 months out.
Turns out the real issue was never the phone. It was that I never built a replacement habit for what I was cutting out.
Verdict: strict phone restriction, scored 54.5/70 vs. 37/70 for staying flexible — testing it for 7 days starting now.
Built the entire report as a single interactive HTML dashboard — animated scoring bars, premortem, everything — with Claude AI.
Sometimes the best productivity hack isn't a new app. It's making yourself actually confront the decision you've been avoiding.
Following this for 7 days. Results next week. 🔁
