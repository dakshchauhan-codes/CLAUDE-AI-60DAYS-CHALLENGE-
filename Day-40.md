🌱 Day 40 of my #60DayClaudeAIChallenge 

Personal Mental Wellness Coach
Built a self-contained wellness check-in app that turns daily mood, stress, energy, and sleep inputs into a personalized, AI-generated wellness report — all running client-side, no backend required.
What it does:
✅ Interactive sliders for Mood, Stress & Energy (1-10)
✅ Sleep hours + journal reflection input
✅ Auto-generates a Wellness Score with a plain-language summary
✅ Personalized recommendations, a 24-hour action plan, and a 7-day tracking plan
✅ "Claude Coaching Chat" — a built-in conversational check-in
The design intent here was simple: most wellness apps either overwhelm you with data or feel clinical. I wanted something that feels more like a quick, honest conversation with a coach — check in, get a score, get 2-3 things you can actually do today.
Tech: single-file HTML/JS, no external dependencies, fully client-side.
This is part of my ongoing challenge to build one AI-powered tool a day using Claude — sharing the process, the wins, and the occasional refactor.
